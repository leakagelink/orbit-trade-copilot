## src/lib rules
- Exchange account-type preferences use spot/futures/both metadata; owner-authenticated edits never enable futures execution or alter keys — selection is not trading authority.
- Exchange I/O goes through `src/lib/exchange-adapters.server.ts` (Web Crypto HMAC, fetch only) and indicator math lives in pure `src/lib/indicators.ts` — Workers-safe and testable.
- Backtests use prior-bar signals and next-open/gap-aware fills in `backtest.ts`; persist full config/candle manifests and ledgers — historical results remain reproducible, not live qualification.
- Bot mutations and connection verification are server-authorized; engine reads bind owners, uncertain fills retain original intents, and inactive bots still manage exits — prevents browser promotion and blind order retries.
- Independent evaluations consume canonical-hashed historical evidence and remain append-only; only versioned admin-authored anchors yield experimental historical utilities, while missing validation never yields a composite or promotion — prevents agent self-grading.
- Evaluation reports retain the scoring policy snapshot and distinguish missing policy, sample floors and independent evidence; observed drawdown remains descriptive and invalid arithmetic yields no utility — keeps historical diagnostics auditable without granting trading authority.
- Paper research math is pure and forward-only, fills from completed daily bars after explicit confirmation, conservatively resolves ambiguous stops, and includes user cost assumptions — avoids historical look-ahead and fabricated live fills.
- Research allowances are atomically claimed before inference; separate immutable paper and emergency-event records never authorize orders or wallet changes — bounds usage and isolates advisory evidence.
- Independent evaluation replays frozen simulator evidence; backtest results and measured research-stage metrics are server-written append-only, and provider costs stay unknown without billing evidence — prevents recomputed fabricated ledgers and invented costs.
- New positions retain entry order IDs and quote-valued fees; spot gross PnL subtracts entry/exit fees once, with unknown legacy costs retained for reconciliation — prevents omitted opening costs.
- Binance production candles and history retry the official data-only host when the primary feed fails; testnet and signed/order endpoints never switch hosts — keeps charts available without changing venues or execution.
- Arbitrage math lives in pure `src/lib/arbitrage.ts`; authenticated highest-tier functions use `arbitrage.server.ts` for server-fetched depth and prefunded legs. Each run and both order intents are durably claimed before submission; ambiguous fills lock further runs for review, never blind retry or transfer funds. AI reviews are advisory; live execution requires user confirmation because cross-exchange orders are not atomic.
- Arbitrage reconciliation is lookup-only by durable client order IDs, with a cooling period and compare-and-set persistence; missing, open, partial or unequal fills keep the lock — prevents blind resubmission and racing active execution. Base fees adjust inventory; only quote fees are additionally deducted from actual cash flows.
- Manual trades go through `src/lib/manual.server.ts` (open/close shared by UI server fns, engine tick and emergency stop); forex/commodities run on OANDA (`oanda*` in exchange-adapters, apiKey=token, apiSecret=account ID), paper SL/TP and OANDA broker-close sync run in `manageManualPositions` each tick.
- Scalps are manual positions flagged `is_scalp` (positions + trades) with `max_hold_until`; rules live in pure `src/lib/scalping.ts`, new entries require highest-tier access and exits run through `checkManualPosition` (engine tick + owner-authenticated `checkScalps` polling) — one exit path for manual trades.
- USD-M Futures use separate `futures*.ts` and `binance-futures.server.ts` paths, dispatch by market_type, and owner-bound credentials; only one-way isolated symbols are supported and broker settings are never silently changed — prevents Spot/Futures confusion.
- Futures entries claim orders before submission and require broker-held algo stops/targets; interrupted entries/exits are lookup-only, missing protection closes reduce-only, and incomplete fee/funding evidence keeps recovery open — prevents duplicate exposure and fabricated PnL.
- Futures live entry requires service-only testnet certification plus explicit owner margin/leverage/loss limits and separate live opt-in; AI cannot certify or relax them — keeps untested derivatives execution locked.
