# Futures Paper Engine — accounting review against draft 0046 (Phase 4E, Workstream C)

Scope: `src/lib/futures-paper-engine.ts` (Phase 4D, SHA-256 `588d782d…a263c3a9`, **unchanged in Phase 4E**),
`src/lib/futures-paper-ledger-v2.ts` (payload builder, unchanged) and draft 0046 `futures_paper_commit_bar_v2`
(unchanged). Nothing was changed to resolve these findings: every resolution below changes behaviour or policy and
needs owner approval. Findings C1, C6 and C7 are pinned by executable characterization tests
(`src/test/phase4e-accounting-characterization.test.ts`); they assert CURRENT behaviour and must be rewritten when an
approved fix lands.

Severity: **High** = a buggy or hostile worker could exceed a limit the database is meant to guarantee; **Medium** = the
engine and the database can disagree and the account takes a deterministic but surprising path; **Low** = definitional
difference, documented.

| # | Mismatch | Where | Risk | Severity | Proposed resolution (needs approval) | Required tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Acceptance vs fill reserved risk.** At acceptance the Risk Engine gets `usedLoss = strategyLoss` (this account's closed losses only). At fill, the engine and 0046 add reserved open risk of every open position of the strategy and losses/reserved risk in the owner's other accounts. | engine `stepFuturesPaper` intents block (`strategy: { budget, usedLoss }`) vs `fillRefusal` and 0046 lines 344–354 | A second same-strategy entry is accepted at the close and cancelled at the next open (`STRATEGY_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`). Safe (never over-reserves) but wastes acceptances and biases acceptance statistics. **Characterized.** | Medium | Feed closed losses + reserved open risk + pending remainders' risk + external usage into acceptance; same definitions as fill. Changes sizing → approval. | Acceptance refuses when reserved + new risk > budget; acceptance and fill agree for 1, 2, N pending entries; cross-account usage at acceptance. |
| C2 | **Pending exposure vs filled exposure.** Acceptance exposure = positions at mark close + pending remainders at mark close. Fill-time exposure (engine and 0046) = positions after the fill at mark open; other pending remainders are not counted. | engine intents block (`exposure`) vs `fillRefusal`; 0046 lines 375–378 | Each pending entry passes its own fill check; the sum of all pending remainders is never checked at fill. Acceptance overstates exposure relative to fill. Bounded by `maxPositions` (one position per symbol). | Low | Pick ONE definition (owner). Option (a) count pending remainders in both; (b) positions only in both. | Equality of acceptance/fill exposure for the chosen definition; two pending entries filling in one candle. |
| C3 | **Missing-mark fallbacks at acceptance.** In single-symbol `stepFuturesPaper` (legacy/offline mode) a held symbol without a supplied mark is valued at its entry price for unrealized P&L/exposure and a pending remainder at **0** for exposure and reserved margin. Fill checks refuse instead (`MARK_MISSING`). | engine intents block (`allMarks[...] ?? q.entryPrice`, `?? 0`; `reservedMargin`) | Acceptance can understate exposure and overstate available margin. Not reachable through `stepFuturesPaperCandle` (every held symbol must have a candle), which is the only entry point the worker design allows. | Medium (legacy path) | Refuse acceptance with INSUFFICIENT_DATA when any held/pending symbol has no mark; or forbid the single-symbol entry point outside tests. | Acceptance with a missing held-symbol mark is refused; worker uses only `stepFuturesPaperCandle` (import test). |
| C4 | **Mark close vs mark open.** Acceptance uses mark **close** of candle N (stop distance, sizing, exposure, daily loss). The fill uses trade **open** of N+1 for price and mark **open** for re-checks. | engine intents block vs pending-fill block; `stepFuturesPaperCandle` marks = mark opens | A gap between close and next open changes notional, margin and unrealized P&L; the fill may be refused (exposure/free margin/daily loss) after acceptance. Stop distance is fixed at acceptance and re-anchored to the fill price, so reserved risk per unit is unchanged. Safe direction (re-checked). | Low | Keep (documented), or re-size at fill (changes behaviour; approval). | Gap-up/gap-down fills: refusal codes deterministic; reserved risk = qty × stopDist + qty × fill × cost. |
| C5 | **Strategy budget source.** The engine uses `cfg.strategyBudgets[strategy]` (per-account config); 0046 uses the owner's `strategy_risk_budgets` row. They can differ (the Phase 4D concurrency test runs with config 100 vs table 12). | engine `fillRefusal` (`cfg.strategyBudgets`) vs 0046 lines 345–346 | When the table is lower, the engine fills and the database refuses; `planCommitRefusal` recomputes into a cancellation (deterministic, no stall). When the table is higher, the engine refuses fills the database would allow. Acceptance uses the config value too. | Medium | Worker must load the budget from the table into the engine context on every candle (single source), or 0046 refuses a payload whose account config budget differs from the table. | Config ≠ table: engine refuses with the table value; budget lowered between candles. |
| C6 | **0046 trusts the worker's `risk_amount`.** The reserved risk of an entry fill is computed by the payload builder (`qty × stopDist + qty × price × cost`, cost passed by the caller) and only checked `≥ 0` by 0046. The stop distance is not in the ledger, so the database cannot recompute it. | `futures-paper-ledger-v2.ts` line 30; 0046 lines 109, 117, 327, 352 | A buggy or hostile worker can understate risk (e.g. 0) and pass the cumulative strategy budget. Also the cost passed to the builder may differ from `cfg.limits.roundTripCostPct`. **Characterized:** a fill with true risk ≈ 10 USDT is refused under a 1 USDT budget when stated honestly and ACCEPTED when stated as 0. | **High** | Successor of 0046: store the stop distance on the accepted order and on each fill, freeze the account's cost assumption in a column, and recompute `risk_amount` in the database (refuse on mismatch). | Understated / overstated / missing risk refused; recomputation equals the engine to 1e-9; partial fills sum to the position's reserve. |
| C7 | **No acceptance precondition for a fill.** 0046 does not require a prior `order_accepted` event for the proposal, nor Σ fills ≤ accepted quantity. Pending entries live only in the opaque state JSON. | 0046 `entry_fill` branch | A pending entry injected into the state (never sized by the Risk Engine) can fill as long as the fill-time limits pass; per-trade loss and leverage limits applied at acceptance are bypassed. **Characterized.** | **High** | Successor of 0046: record accepted quantity, leverage and stop distance on `order_accepted`; require it for the first fill; cap Σ fills by the accepted quantity; refuse fills after `entry_cancelled`. | Fill without acceptance refused; over-fill refused; fill after cancellation refused; leverage differs from accepted refused. |
| C8 | **Daily loss and the UTC reset.** Both sides bucket by the candle's open time (`floor(bar_t / 86 400 000)`), not by event time. A time-stop exit stamped at the close of the 23:59 candle (00:00 next day) counts on the earlier day. Acceptance on the last candle of a day uses that day's loss; its fill at 00:00 runs after the reset. | engine `day`/`dayCash`; 0046 line 340 | Definitional; engine and database agree. An entry refused at 23:59 for daily loss is not re-proposed automatically; an entry accepted at 23:59 near the limit fills after the reset. | Low | Owner confirms "UTC day of the candle open" as the definition (decision register §2). | Exit at 23:59 candle counted on day D; fill at 00:00 after a day-D breach allowed; state `dayCash` equals the ledger sum after restart. |
| C9 | **Multi-symbol ordering.** `stepFuturesPaperCandle` steps symbols alphabetically; each symbol's intents are evaluated right after that symbol's own step. A BTCUSDT intent is therefore sized before ETHUSDT's funding, fills and exits of the same candle are applied. 0046 processes events in the same payload order, so they agree — but results depend on symbol names. | engine `stepFuturesPaperCandle` loop | Acceptance for an earlier symbol can use an account state that changes later in the same candle (e.g. a later-symbol liquidation raising today's loss). Fill checks are consistent with 0046. | Medium | Two-phase candle: (1) funding, fills and exits for all symbols; (2) all intents against the post-exit account. Behaviour change → approval. | Order invariance: renaming/permuting symbols gives the same decisions; same-candle liquidation blocks a later intent. |
| C10 | **Cross-account and concurrent commits.** 0046 locks the account row, then every strategy budget the payload's fills consume, in strategy order; the ledger view of other accounts is read under those locks. The engine's `externalStrategy` is read by the worker before the commit and can be stale. | 0046 lines 287, 309–313 | Stale usage makes the engine optimistic; the database refuses and the refusal is planned into a cancellation (real-PostgreSQL test, Phase 4D). No double reservation. A worker that locks two accounts in one transaction could deadlock. | Low | Worker: one account per transaction; re-read usage after a refusal (already in the design). | Existing Phase 4D two-account race (passes, 3 runs); deadlock-free ordering test when two accounts of one owner commit fills of two strategies in opposite order. |

## Not changed

No engine, payload-builder or migration change was made for any finding; no owner-approved or unapproved policy value
was changed; the proposed values (2% liquidation buffer, 5 s mark age, 60 s proposal age, 0.13% round-trip cost) remain
inputs supplied by the caller and are **NOT APPROVED** (decision register).
